# Android Device Matrix

Test at least:
- Low-tier Android device: constrained RAM/CPU, small screen.
- Mid-tier Android device: representative primary audience.
- High-tier Android device: high refresh rate and larger display.
- Small and large font settings.
- Gesture navigation and three-button navigation.
- Slow network, airplane mode, captive portal, intermittent reconnect.
- Low storage and app process termination.
- Portrait and landscape for approved games.
- Reduced motion, mute and haptics disabled.

Record device model, OS, app build, game ID, seed, steps, expected and actual result.
