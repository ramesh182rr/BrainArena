# Privacy, Safety and Claims

## Product language
Use "puzzle", "recreational mental fitness", "in-app performance", "game mastery" and "personalised challenges".

Avoid "diagnose", "treat", "prevent dementia", "improve IQ", "clinically proven cognitive improvement" or guaranteed real-world outcomes unless independently substantiated and legally reviewed.

## Data minimisation
- Guest profile can use a random local identifier.
- Account link should be optional.
- Collect no precise location, contacts or health records.
- Avoid date of birth; if an adult confirmation is required, use a neutral age gate and minimise stored data.
- Provide privacy policy and data deletion workflow.
- Account deletion must be discoverable in-app and through an external web resource when account creation is supported.
- Disclose analytics, ads, purchase and sync data accurately.

## Ads
- Rewarded ad only after explicit user tap.
- State reward and conditions before the ad.
- If ad unavailable or skipped, preserve normal gameplay.
- Verify reward server-side where provider supports server-side verification.
- Do not target sensitive attributes.
- Audience is 18+, but still review applicable ad-network and Play policies.

## Disclaimer draft
"BrainArena Solo is a recreational puzzle and entertainment app. Scores and recommendations describe performance within BrainArena games and are not medical, diagnostic or clinical assessments. The app is not intended to prevent, diagnose, treat or cure any medical condition."
