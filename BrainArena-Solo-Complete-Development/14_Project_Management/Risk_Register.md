# Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 games overrun solo capacity | High | High | Vertical slices, phased batches, quality gates | Developer |
| Similarity to existing game | Medium | High | Originality review, distinct interaction grammar, legal review | Developer |
| Procedural generator defects | Medium | High | Solvers, property tests, fixed seeds, fallback content | Developer |
| Free hosting cold starts | High | Medium | Offline-first client, paid upgrade trigger | Developer |
| Sync data loss/conflicts | Medium | High | Append-only events, idempotency, recovery tests | Developer |
| Ads violate policy or UX | Medium | High | Rewarded-only, consent, server-side verification | Developer |
| Weak retention | High | High | Playtests, cohort metrics, improve game quality | Developer |
| Scientific claims overreach | Low | High | Recreational positioning and copy review | Developer |
